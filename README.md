# RefurTrace — Digital Product Passport Platform for Refurbished Assets

## Transforming Refurbished Asset Trust Through Digital Traceability

RefurTrace is an enterprise-grade Digital Product Passport (DPP) platform built on ServiceNow to provide end-to-end lifecycle transparency for refurbished electronic and industrial assets.

The platform enables manufacturers, service providers, resellers, buyers, and compliance teams to securely track asset authenticity, ownership history, repair records, warranty details, and compliance certifications using QR-enabled digital traceability.

RefurTrace combines workflow automation, lifecycle governance, fraud prevention, and sustainability tracking into a unified platform.

---

# Problem Statement

The refurbished asset industry faces major challenges:

- Buyers cannot verify whether products are genuine or safely repaired
- Ownership history is often missing or manipulated
- Repair and maintenance records are fragmented
- Fraudulent resale activities are increasing
- Regulatory compliance and sustainability tracking are becoming mandatory
- Existing systems lack transparent lifecycle governance

As global industries move toward sustainability and circular economy models, Digital Product Passport systems are becoming increasingly important.

RefurTrace addresses these problems by building a trusted digital identity for every asset.

---

# Solution Overview

RefurTrace provides a secure and scalable platform for managing the complete lifecycle of refurbished assets.

The system creates a Digital Product Passport for every asset and tracks:

- Manufacturing details
- Ownership transfers
- Service and repair history
- Warranty records
- Compliance certifications
- Fraud reports
- Verification workflows

Every asset can be verified using QR-based digital validation.
